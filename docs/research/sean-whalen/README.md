# Sean Whalen research packet

Last researched: 2026-08-02

This folder is a conservative, identity-checked research packet for the poet Sean Whalen who lives near Pilot Mound in rural Boone County, Iowa. It is intended to support a future writer website without mixing in work by other people named Sean Whalen.

## What is here

- [`profile.md`](./profile.md): readable identity dossier, bibliography, style analysis, image leads, exclusions, and website guidance.
- [`works.json`](./works.json): structured bibliography for later import into a site or CMS.
- [`small-ecologies.json`](./small-ecologies.json): complete poem-level index of Sean's 2004 Iowa State M.A. thesis, including thesis pages and earlier-publication credits.
- [`media.json`](./media.json): image references and permission status. No third-party image has been copied into the repository.

## Current coverage

- 82 unique works with publicly verifiable titles, represented by 83 confirmed title forms because one poem was substantially revised and retitled.
- 46 poems indexed from the 2004 M.A. thesis _Small ecologies_; 40 are exact-title additions beyond the earlier packet.
- 4 separately recorded later appearances, revisions, or republications of already indexed work.
- 4 publication credits for which a title or definitive issue remains unavailable, with confidence recorded per item.
- 1 still-unresolved forthcoming claim.
- 3 published portrait references. One is a 1638×2048 professional studio portrait, but none is licensed for reuse yet.

This is best described as **all publicly verifiable work located as of the research date**, not Sean's complete private bibliography. Several older journals and recent print-only issues are not fully searchable online.

## Identity gate

A work was admitted to the title-level index only when its source connected it to the Pilot Mound writer directly or when it fit the established bibliographic chain for his early work. The recurring fingerprint is:

- Pilot Mound or rural Boone County, Iowa;
- retired health-and-safety professional;
- volunteer fire chief;
- M.A. in Creative Writing from Iowa State University; and
- a publication history linking early work in _Flyway_, _The Midwest Quarterly_, and _Mid-American Review_ to his later publications.

The strongest anchors are the user-supplied [Stone Poetry Quarterly author page](https://stonepoetryjournal.com/sean-whalen/), Sean's first-person [Gyroscope Review profile](https://www.gyroscopereview.com/2026/04/poet-pick-sean-whalen/), the Iowa State repository record for [_Small ecologies_](https://dr.lib.iastate.edu/handle/20.500.12876/69962), and an independent [Pilot Mound fire-department item](https://pilotmound.org/2021/02/01/pilot-mound-gets-a-aed/).

## Copyright and privacy rules

- The packet stores titles, bibliographic metadata, links, and original research notes—not poem text.
- A poem being readable online does not make it free to republish. Use links until Sean confirms that he controls the needed rights and grants website permission.
- The portrait links are references only. The best lead is watermarked and carries Stacy McDonald/The Photician copyright metadata. Ask Sean and the photographer for an unwatermarked original, an exact credit line, and written web-use permission.
- Do not infer that a poem's speaker or events are autobiographical.
- Do not retain or publish residential addresses, personal phone numbers, or data-broker records.

## Updating the packet

When a new source appears, match it against at least two identity signals, add a canonical publication event rather than a duplicate work, record whether it is a first publication or reprint, and update the counts above. Forthcoming credits should not be promoted to published works until a title and issue or publication page are visible.
